Privacy Policy

Speaker & Headphone Test () · Last updated 05 August 2026

This policy explains what Speaker & Headphone Test does with information when you use it. It covers only this app.

What the app uses, and why

  • Microphone. Used to measure the sound level around you. The audio is analysed live on your device to produce a level reading; nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Tracking permission. If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier your device provides is used to attribute your install to the campaign that led you here. If you decline, attribution falls back to Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork and no advertising identifier is read.
  • Settings you choose. Your preferences — selected language, theme, sound options and progress through the app — are stored on your device only.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are billed by Apple. We never see or receive your payment card, and Apple shares only whether a purchase succeeded.

The app asks for tracking permission on first launch. You can change your answer at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking on your device.

Who else processes data

These services act on our behalf and only receive what their function needs:

  • Google Fonts. Serves the typefaces used in the interface. Receives your IP address when a font is fetched.
  • Apphud. Manages subscriptions and purchases. Receives a pseudonymous user identifier, device and app version, country and purchase history so your subscription can be restored on your other devices.
  • Tenjin. Measures which advertisement or link brought you to the app. Receives device and app identifiers, install and session events, and the country your device reports.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not ask for your name, address or phone number.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you.

How long data is kept

Settings and progress stay on your device until you delete the app. Subscription and attribution records are kept by the services above for as long as they need them to provide the service, and are deleted on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

You can ask what data relates to you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to kristjan@chritech.site and we will respond within 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything held on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
