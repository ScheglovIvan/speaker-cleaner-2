Common questions

  • A purchase did not unlock. Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not unlock, email us the Apple ID region you bought from.
  • How do I cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the App Store, tap your account photo, then Subscriptions.
  • Something is not working. Email us what you were doing when it happened — a screenshot helps.
